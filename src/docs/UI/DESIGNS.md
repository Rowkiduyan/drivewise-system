# DriveWise UI Design

## Design Philosophy

DriveWise is a modern, friendly, and professional fleet management system.

The interface should prioritize:

- Readability
- Consistency
- Simplicity
- Maintainability

Avoid unnecessary visual clutter.

---

## Theme

Background

- White

Overall Style

- Modern
- Friendly
- Professional

Do not use:

- Heavy gradients
- Glassmorphism
- Neumorphism
- Excessive shadows

---

## Cards

Cards should be:

- Flat
- White background
- Minimal border
- Small rounded corners

---

## Buttons

Buttons should:

- Be rounded
- Match the active role's primary color when appropriate

Danger buttons should always be red.

---

## Tables

Tables should:

- Have sticky headers
- Have hover effects
- Remain readable on all screen sizes

---

## Forms

Forms should:

- Have labels above inputs
- Have placeholders
- Mark required fields with a red asterisk (*)

---

## Icons

Do not use icons unless explicitly requested.
